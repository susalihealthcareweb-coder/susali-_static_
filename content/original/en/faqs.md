# Everything you need / to know.
Categories: All · General · Patient Services · Doctors & Partners

### [General] What is SUSALI HEALTH CARE SERVICES PVT LTD?
SUSALI is a healthcare coordination platform designed to simplify access to healthcare services by connecting patients with doctors, hospitals, diagnostic centres, and other healthcare service providers through an organized and patient-focused approach.

### [General] What services does SUSALI provide?
SUSALI facilitates healthcare coordination services, including patient registration, doctor and hospital referrals, appointment coordination, diagnostic service coordination, healthcare information management, and follow-up support.

### [General] What is the primary objective of SUSALI?
Our objective is to make healthcare more accessible, organized, and convenient by creating reliable connections between patients and healthcare providers.

### [Patient Services] How can I register with SUSALI?
Patients can register through our website by providing the required basic information. A registered profile can help organize their healthcare information and support future referral and coordination requests, subject to the platform's available features.

### [Patient Services] How can SUSALI help me find a doctor or hospital?
SUSALI helps coordinate connections with suitable doctors and hospitals based on the patient's healthcare requirements, location, and available service options.

### [Patient Services] Can SUSALI help me book a doctor’s appointment?
Yes. SUSALI can assist with appointment coordination by communicating with relevant healthcare providers and helping patients understand the available appointment options.

### [Patient Services] Can I access my previous healthcare information?
Where supported by our platform, registered patients can access or manage their available healthcare information and records. Access depends on the information recorded, the platform's features, and applicable privacy permissions.

### [Patient Services] Does SUSALI provide emergency medical services?
SUSALI may assist with healthcare coordination, but it is not a substitute for emergency medical services. For a medical emergency in India, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department.

### [Doctors & Partners] Can hospitals and doctors collaborate with SUSALI?
Eligible doctors, hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centres and other healthcare providers can contact SUSALI to explore collaboration opportunities.

### [Doctors & Partners] How can a hospital partner with SUSALI?
Healthcare institutions can contact the SUSALI team to discuss partnership requirements, referral coordination, service availability, documentation and agreed terms.
