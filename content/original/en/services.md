## Philosophy
_OUR SERVICE PHILOSOPHY_
# One Patient. Many / Moments of Care.
Healthcare needs can change over time. SUSALI brings important parts of these interactions into a continuing patient profile. Our services connect people, processes, and available information while treating hospitals and doctors retain responsibility for clinical care.

### SERVICE 01 — Patient Registration & Digital Profile
A familiar place for your healthcare information. Rather than registering again for every health concern, patients continue using the same account as new referrals and treatment episodes occur.

## Journey
_HOW THE JOURNEY CONNECTS_
# From First Registration to / Continued Care.
### 01 Begin Your Journey
Register with SUSALI and establish your individual healthcare profile.

### 02 Seek Assistance
When a health concern arises, begin the appropriate healthcare consultation process.

### 03 Connect Through SUSALI
Log in to your profile and submit the necessary referral and healthcare information.

### 04 Hospital Appointment
The appointment is coordinated with the relevant hospital, and details are made available to you.

### 05 Consult Your Doctor
Your treating professional evaluates your condition and recommends appropriate care.

### 06 Receive Care
Your care may include consultation, diagnostics, hospitalization or surgical treatment.

### 07 Medical Story Updated
Authorized staff upload relevant medical documents and treatment information to your profile.

### 08 Stay Connected
Access previous records, review follow-ups and continue using your profile for future visits.
