## Hero
_THE PEOPLE BEHIND CONNECTED CARE_
# Better Healthcare Connections Are / Built Together.
Patients, doctors, hospitals, referral authorities, and coordination teams each play a different role in the healthcare journey. SUSALI supports the connections that help these participants work within a clearer process.

## Why
_WHY THE HEALTHCARE NETWORK MATTERS_
# Healthcare Works Better When Connections / Work Together.
For a patient, healthcare may feel like a series of separate places and conversations. Bringing the information surrounding these moments together helps patients better understand their healthcare journey.

Our network approach focuses on coordination and information continuity between participants, supporting a clearer process for a more connected patient experience.

## Ecosystem
### Patients — At the Heart
Manage referral submissions, view appointment details, and access uploaded medical documents through one continuing profile.

### Doctors — Clinical Expertise
Doctors assess needs, diagnose conditions, provide treatment, and guide continued care. SUSALI supports the coordination framework surrounding these interactions.

### Hospitals — Professional Care
Participating hospitals provide medical consultations, tests, and treatment while authorized staff upload relevant treatment documents to SUSALI.

### Referral Authorities — The Pathway
Initial healthcare processes issue required referrals, connecting authorized referral information with the next stage of hospital care.

### Coordination Teams — Practical Steps
Teams help organize administrative communication between referrals, hospitals, and arranged appointments.

### Diagnostic Services — Supporting Care
Laboratory tests and scans inform clinical decisions. Available uploaded findings remain associated with the patient profile.

### Medical Camps — Community Reach
Organizing health camps to provide initial screening, medical awareness, and connecting communities to the SUSALI healthcare network.
