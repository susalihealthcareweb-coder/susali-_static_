## Hero
_THE STORY BEHIND SUSALI_
# Built to Connect. / Inspired by Care.
SUSALI is built on a simple belief: healthcare becomes easier to navigate when patients, doctors, hospitals, and the people coordinating care are better connected.

## Who we are
_WHO WE ARE_
# A Bridge Between / Patients and Doctors.
A healthcare journey rarely follows a single straight line. It may begin with a new health concern, continue through a referral, lead to a hospital appointment, and require tests, treatment, or another consultation.

Where should we go? What information is required? When is the appointment? Where are the previous medical reports? SUSALI exists to help connect these moments.

We are not a substitute for a doctor, nor do we make medical decisions. Our contribution is to support a more organized experience surrounding professional healthcare.

## Mission
_OUR MISSION_
### Making the Journey Around Healthcare Clearer.
Our mission is to bridge the gap between patients and doctors through thoughtful healthcare coordination, organized medical information, and accessible digital patient profiles.

## Vision
_OUR VISION_
### A Future of Connected Healthcare Experiences.
We envision a healthcare environment where patients can access their available medical history, healthcare stakeholders communicate through clearer pathways, and different treatment episodes remain part of one continuous story.

## Values
_OUR CORE VALUES_
# The principles that guide / our commitment.
### Compassion
Every medical record belongs to a person, and every appointment may matter deeply to a family.

### Clarity
People should be able to understand the purpose of each step, the information required, and where to seek assistance.

### Connection
Better coordination depends on respectful collaboration between patients, healthcare providers, referral authorities, and support teams.

### Continuity
Available previous medical information should remain linked to the patient's existing profile across different healthcare episodes.

### Responsibility
Healthcare information requires careful handling, appropriate access, and accurate communication.

### Continuous Improvement
Making an ordinary healthcare process simpler, clearer, and more dependable creates valuable improvements.
