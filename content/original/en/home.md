## Welcome
_WELCOME TO SUSALI_
# Healthcare Begins with / People.
Every patient has a unique story. Behind every appointment is a person who needs clear information and a reliable connection to professional medical care.

SUSALI is built around this human side of healthcare. We aim to simplify the journey by connecting patients, authorized referral pathways, healthcare coordination teams, and participating hospitals.

Instead of treating each hospital visit as an isolated event, SUSALI brings available healthcare information into one continuing patient profile.

## Services
_OUR HEALTHCARE SERVICES_
# Supporting the Moments / That Matter.
Good healthcare coordination involves much more than arranging an appointment. It requires clear communication, organized information, and continued access.

### Patient Registration & Digital Profile
A familiar place for your healthcare information. Rather than registering again for every health concern, patients continue using the same account as new referrals and treatment episodes occur.

_One continuing patient account with an organized history._

[View details]

### Patient–Doctor Connection & Referral Coordination
Supporting the steps that lead to professional care. When a new concern arises, patients submit relevant referral details through their profile to connect with the coordination process.

_Digital referral submission linked to coordination._

[View details]

### Hospital Appointment Coordination
Making the next step easier to understand. Once required referral information is submitted, an appointment is coordinated with a participating hospital through the applicable process.

_Coordinated hospital visits & clear visit details._

[View details]

### Hospital Treatment Journey
Connected information across outpatient consultations, inpatient admission, laboratory tests, diagnostic imaging, or surgical procedures.

_Outpatient, Inpatient, Diagnostics & Surgical info._

[View details]

### Digital Medical Documents
Important medical documents connected to the person they belong to. Authorized hospital staff upload available prescriptions, investigation results, scan reports, and discharge details.

_Prescriptions, lab reports & scans in one place._

[View details]

### Continued Care & Follow-up Information
Because healthcare can continue after the visit. When doctor recommendations are recorded, patients can refer to follow-up details alongside previous medical documents.

# A Clearer Healthcare Journey, / Step by Step.
Every healthcare journey is different, but clear information and coordinated processes make the experience easier to navigate.

### PHASE 01 / 04 — Begin Journey
**Register & Refer**
Establish your profile and initiate the appropriate healthcare consultation.

### PHASE 02 / 04 — Hospital Visit
**Care & Consultation**

### PHASE 03 / 04 — Medical Records
**Information Updated**
Authorized staff upload relevant medical documents and treatment information to your profile.
